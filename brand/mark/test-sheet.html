Clear space and minimum size

  • Clear space on every side: one quarter of the mark's height (dashed box = mark bounds, outer square = minimum clear area).
  • Full mark (with the light cut): 32 px and larger.
  • Small mark (no cut): 16 to 24 px: favicon, sidebar, menu bar, toolbar.
  • Minimum size: 16 px on screen, 6 mm in print.
  • Colour: gold #F2A900 on light or dark; ink #17181B or white when one colour is required. Never recolour the star in other hues, outline it, add effects or rotate it.
  • The halo exists only inside the app icon tile. The mark itself is always flat.
