SEO Basics: star mark

Direction H, "Glint", approved by Lars on 2026-11-05. A full five-point star with filleted tips and inner corners, and one small four-point twinkle in its upper-right crook: the sparkle of the 🌟 reference, kept to a single accent. From 32 px up it is the full mark; at 16 to 24 px the small variant sets a larger twinkle slightly further out so it survives.

Clear space and minimum size

  • Clear space on every side: one quarter of the mark's height (dashed box = mark bounds, outer square = minimum clear area).
  • Full mark: 32 px and larger.
  • Small mark (larger twinkle): 16 to 24 px: favicon, sidebar, menu bar, toolbar, and the 16/32 px app icon tiles.
  • Minimum size: 16 px on screen, 6 mm in print.
  • Colour: gold #F2A900 on light or dark; ink #17181B or white when one colour is required. Star and twinkle always share one colour. Never recolour, outline, rotate, add effects, or move or drop the twinkle.
  • The glow and the top-lit fill exist only inside the app icon tile. The mark itself is always flat, one colour.
