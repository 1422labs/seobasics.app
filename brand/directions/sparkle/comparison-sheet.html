SEO Basics: star mark, sparkle round

Four new directions after the refined Notch was declined ("more sparkling, like the emoji, and more professional"). Every mark is one flat path in a 100 × 100 space, rendered here at true pixel size (no scaling). The smallest sizes use each direction's optical variant. The macOS tile (dark; light at 128) adds a soft gold glow and a gentle top-lit fill to the star; the marks themselves stay flat, one colour. Lettered E–H to follow the first round (A–D).
